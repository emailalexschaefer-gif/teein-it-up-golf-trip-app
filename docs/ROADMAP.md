# Teein' It Up — Roadmap

This is a sequencing document, not a commitment. Items move between
columns as real usage (starting with Friday's field test) produces
evidence about what actually matters next. See `VISION.md` for why any
of this matters, and `SOCIAL_GOLF_MVP_STATUS.md` / `TESTING.md` for
exactly what's built today.

---

## Now (Friday blockers)

Whatever directly threatens the checklist above. As of this document,
that's real-device confirmation of the scoring screen's static/scrollable
behaviour, the Social Golf migration actually being applied to
production, and end-to-end confirmation of the Playing Nine → scoring →
reconciliation → results chain with real Front/Back Nine hole numbers.

## October launch

Work aimed at a broader, less hands-on-testing-dependent release:
Awaiting Scores / Ready to Finalise round states, Finalise Round UI
polish, a fuller My Round content audit, general stability and
performance work already scoped in `PERFORMANCE_NATIVE_FEEL_VISION.md`.

## Social Golf

Beyond the MVP: season ladder, rivalries, streaks, head-to-head records,
personal records over time. Explicitly not started — see the Future
Roadmap section of `SOCIAL_GOLF_MVP_STATUS.md` for the parked list.

## My Golf ecosystem

The longer-range shape of the product across all three pillars: My
Trips (accommodation, itinerary, travel timeline, trip memories), My
Events (sponsors, charity, corporate branding, registration), and
Moments (story timeline, AI highlights, albums, video). Nothing here is
scoped or started.

---

## How to use this document

New ideas get written down here, in the column that matches their
actual urgency — not automatically in "Now" just because they came up
during a conversation about something urgent. That's the discipline this
document exists to protect: Friday's field test should only ever be
blocked by things that are genuinely Friday blockers.
