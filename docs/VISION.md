# Teein' It Up — Vision

---

## Vision

We bring people together through golf.

Golf has always been a reason for people to gather — a trip with old
friends, a Friday afternoon with the same two mates, a corporate day
that's really about the relationships in the room. The golf itself is
often not even the main point. The gathering is. Teein' It Up exists to
make that gathering effortless, so the people organising it can spend
their energy on the people, not the logistics.

---

## Mission

Enable golfers to effortlessly organise, experience and remember every
round of golf.

Three words carry the weight here, deliberately:

- **Organise** — before a single ball is hit. Groups, tee times,
  handicaps, invitations. The unglamorous work that determines whether
  the day goes smoothly.
- **Experience** — during the round itself. Scoring that keeps up with
  someone walking down a fairway, not fighting them for attention.
  Reconciliation that resolves disagreements without an argument on the
  17th tee.
- **Remember** — after the round ends. A result that means something, a
  photo that's easy to find again, a season that has a shape to look
  back on.

Most golf apps optimise for one of these. We think the real product is
all three, connected.

---

## The Three Pillars

### My Trips
Destination golf. A group of people travelling somewhere to play —
several rounds, one shared trip. This is where Teein' It Up started,
and it remains the deepest, most complete pillar: groups, scoring,
reconciliation, leaderboards, and the trip as a container for the whole
experience.

### My Social
Regular golf. The same faces, the same course (or courses), week after
week, season after season — a standing arrangement rather than a single
trip. Dave & Alex's Friday series is the founding example. This pillar
proves the same underlying engine that runs a week-long trip can just as
well run an ongoing weekly habit, without asking the organiser to set
anything up twice.

### My Events
Corporate days, charity days, societies, and other special-occasion
golf. Different stakes and different audiences than a trip among
friends — sponsors, branding, sometimes registration — but the same
need underneath: get the right people to the right tee time with the
right handicaps, and let the day run itself from there.

---

## North Star

Become every golfer's digital home.

Not a scoring app they open once a year for a big trip, and not a
generic handicap tracker with no memory of *this* group or *this*
season. The measure of success is whether, a year from now, checking
Teein' It Up feels as natural to a golfer as checking their group chat —
because it already knows who they play with, where they've been, and
what happened last time.

## What This Document Is Not

This is not a roadmap and not a technical specification. It doesn't say
what's built or when the next thing ships — see `ROADMAP.md` for that,
and `SOCIAL_GOLF_MVP_STATUS.md` / `TESTING.md` for what actually exists
today. This document exists so that decisions about *what* to build next
can be checked against *why* the product exists in the first place —
including the decision to say no to things that don't serve it.
